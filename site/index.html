Everything the installation team needs

Built around how a fenestration project actually runs, with the checks and records your main contractor expects.

📦

BOQ and P6 planning

Upload the BOQ from Excel, split lines into buildings, levels and elevations, and turn a scope-level P6 programme into a detailed, resourced schedule. Export back to P6 (.xer).

⏱

Realistic schedules

Durations and crews follow standard productivity and the working day left after rest, lunch and prayer breaks you set. Gantt, baseline, lean planner and forecast finish.

📊

Manhour and resource forecast

Forecast at completion, weekly workers needed, and an operational budget set to the lower of estimate and forecast. Management and the estimation manager are alerted when the forecast exceeds the budget.

📤

Delegation with alarms

Pre-start checklists, look-ahead at 30, 7 and 1 day, and task invitations. If the project manager has not delegated within 24 hours, management and the planner are alarmed; unassigned tasks are reported daily.

🧱

QR clock-in with location

Workers scan QR badges to clock in; the day's log clocks the team out. Every scan records the location and alerts the project manager when it is away from site.

✅

QA/QC: WIR, MIR and SOR

Inspection checklists with photos and signatures, WIR packages to the main contractor, material inspection requests, and an automatic site observation report when material is rejected.

📁

Numbered letters and forms

Serial numbering with your own patterns and start numbers, a register with stored copies, and your own Word, Excel or PDF formats filled automatically. Download as a report, a ZIP or one PDF.

💰

IPA, variations and delay claims

Monthly IPA built from approved MIR, WIR and snag approvals, instructions priced into variations, and FIDIC-based delay reports and extension of time letters. Commercial items stay with management and the project manager.

🏆

Monthly KPIs and certificates

Scorecards and ranking for the project manager, site engineers, QA/QC engineers and team leaders. Management reviews, approves officially and issues numbered certificates of recognition.

Log in or request an account

Existing users sign in with the user name and password issued to them. New staff can request an account; Management approves it and confirms your role before you can sign in.

  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and repeated wrong attempts lock the account for a short time.
  • Every action is logged with your name and the time.
  • First sign-in with a temporary password asks you to choose your own.

Main contractor and production department accounts are created by Management.

Your request waits for approval by Management. You will be able to sign in once it is approved.

Questions

Where is my data stored?

In a single SQLite database on your own server (or the host you choose). Management can download a backup that contains the database and a plain SQL dump, and a daily backup is made automatically.

Can I keep my own letter and form layouts?

Yes. Upload a Word, Excel or PDF format for each document type and the app fills it. You also choose the numbering pattern and start number for each type.

Does it send emails and WhatsApp messages?

Yes, when your administrator configures email (SMTP) or WhatsApp. Alarms such as late delegation are checked by the server every 10 minutes, even when nobody is logged in.

Does location tracking need special setup?

Scan locations need the app to be served over https and the team leader to allow location access. You set the site position and the allowed distance in Setup.

Which languages and devices are supported?

English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Filipino, Bengali, Tamil and Chinese, with PC, tablet and phone layouts. It can be installed on a phone like an app.

When will the other modules be available?

Logistics, Production, Materials and Engineering are planned. Their tabs are shown greyed out in the header and become active as each module is released.
